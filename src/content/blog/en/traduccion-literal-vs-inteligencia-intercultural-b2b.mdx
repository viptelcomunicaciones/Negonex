Many exporting and importing companies invest heavily in international trade shows and outbound campaigns, only to lose qualified buyers during the first digital exchange. International business literature referenced in IIT S.A.S.'s business model indicates that roughly **40% of cross-border B2B opportunities for Latin American SMBs** stall at the initial stage due to intercultural communication asymmetries, time-zone delays, and literal translations that undermine corporate credibility.

## Three hidden frictions in global first contact

- **Time-zone gaps:** while commercial teams in Latin America are offline, buyers in Frankfurt, Riyadh, or Tokyo send technical inquiries during their business hours and expect prompt, accurate replies.
- **Register and etiquette mismatches:** the same Spanish commercial message requires ROI-focused directness in the United States, formal precision (*Sie*) in Germany, relationship-first courtesy in the Gulf, and honorific hierarchy (*Keigo*) in Japan.
- **Disconnected CRM and ERP workflows:** when international inquiries sit in isolated chat threads, teams lose traceability over price lists, Incoterms, and lead qualification.
