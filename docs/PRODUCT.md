## Platform

## Users

- **Primary users:** Foreign trade directors, export/import managers, commercial directors, and general managers at Latin American and international B2B exporting/importing SMBs and mid-to-large enterprises.
- **Secondary visitors:** Procurement leads, IT/CRM integration owners, and academic or institutional evaluators reviewing the NegoNEX AI SaaS architecture.
- **Situations & jobs:** Evaluating whether an AI conversational agent can handle cross-border B2B buyer inquiries 24/7 across time zones without committing cultural or hierarchy blunders, confirming CRM/ERP compatibility, estimating total monthly/setup SaaS costs, and requesting a tailored demonstration or self-service Starter onboarding.

## Product Purpose

NegoNEX AI (operated by Intercultural Intelligence Technologies S.A.S. — IIT S.A.S.) eliminates language and intercultural etiquette barriers in international B2B negotiations. It deploys 24/7 multilingual conversational AI agents (+95 languages) trained on each client's technical documentation, price lists, Incoterms, and product catalogs, paired with bidirectional CRM/ERP integration and predictive market analytics, to accelerate response times, qualify international leads (MQL/SQL), and increase cross-border closing rates.

## Positioning

Unlike generic chatbots or literal translation tools that fail on corporate tone, hierarchy, and negotiation pacing, NegoNEX AI combines hybrid LLM orchestration (Claude Sonnet 4.5 + Gemini 2.5 Flash) with curated intercultural etiquette matrices tailored to destination markets (North America, Latin America, Western Europe, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, and East Asia including Japanese Keigo).

## Operating Context

- **Delivery model:** 100% remote cloud SaaS hosted on containerized AWS Lightsail instances with Amazon S3 backups, protected by Cloudflare Pro/Zero Trust WAF and Auth0 MFA, with TLS 1.3 and AES-256 encryption.
- **Channels & integrations:** Customizable Web Widget, official WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Chatwoot CE omnichannel inbox, ERPNext CRM, and bidirectional REST/Webhook connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP, and Odoo (plus optional WeChat/LINE in Enterprise), orchestrated via n8n and Python (FastAPI/LangChain).
- **Analytics (Plan Intelligence):** Python (Pandas, DuckDB, Plotly) pipelines feeding interactive Microsoft Power BI and Google Looker Studio dashboards and quarterly market-intelligence reports.
- **Compliance & guarantees:** GDPR (EU), CCPA (US), Law 1581 of 2012 (Colombia), Enterprise DPA (client data excluded from public model training), 99.9% monthly uptime SLA (10%–25% automatic credit), 30-day setup satisfaction guarantee, 30-day unlimited linguistic calibration, and 15-day cancellation notice on Starter.

## Product Principles

1. **Precision over hype:** Present exact capabilities, pricing, lead times, and contractual terms without unverified claims or generic AI marketing clichés.
2. **Demonstrate intercultural value immediately:** Let B2B visitors compare literal translation against cultural adaptation in seconds via the interactive Sandbox.
3. **Transparency in pricing and scaling:** Show setup fees, monthly subscriptions, overage block rates, and Enterprise upgrade thresholds openly.
4. **Enterprise trust by default:** Highlight Zero Trust perimeter security, DPA data privacy, and regulatory compliance (GDPR, CCPA, Law 1581) clearly for procurement and IT evaluators.
